[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Apex_Lift-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Thegabi9944/apex-lift-workout-hub)

## 🎯 About the project

Apex Lift Workout Hub provides a central space for gym management. You create custom fitness plans, log your daily progress, and monitor your physical gains over time. This platform helps you organize your gym routine so you stay on track with your fitness goals.

## 💻 System requirements

To run Apex Lift on your Windows computer, verify that your machine meets these specifications:

*   **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   **Web Browser:** The latest version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
*   **Memory:** At least 4GB of RAM.
*   **Storage:** 200MB of free disk space for local cache files.
*   **Internet Connection:** A stable connection to sync your workout logs to the cloud.

## ⚙️ Configuration for your first use

Once the installation finishes, the Apex Lift icon appears on your desktop. Double-click the icon to launch the application. The program opens in your default web browser.

Follow these steps to set up your profile:

1.  Enter your email address and create a secure password.
2.  Select your preferred measurement units, such as pounds or kilograms.
3.  Define your primary fitness goals, such as muscle gain, weight loss, or endurance.
4.  Save your profile settings to access your dashboard.

## 📋 Building your first training plan

The platform uses a modular approach to exercise management. Use these steps to build your first routine:

1.  Open the "Plans" tab on the left sidebar.
2.  Click the "Create New Plan" button.
3.  Name your plan, such as "Monday Chest and Triceps."
4.  Select exercises from the library by clicking the "+" button next to the movement name.
5.  Input your target sets, repetitions, and rest periods for each movement.
6.  Click "Save Plan" to add it to your library.

## 📈 Logging your progress

Consistency drives results. Use the dashboard to log your workouts in real time:

1.  Go to the "Today" tab.
2.  Select the plan you created earlier.
3.  Click "Start Workout" to begin your session.
4.  As you complete each set, enter the weight and repetitions performed.
5.  Click the checkmark to log the set as complete.
6.  When you finish the last exercise, click "End Workout" to save the data.

## 🛠️ Managing your gym operations

Apex Lift keeps track of your history so you see your growth over months or years.

*   **View Reports:** Navigate to the "Analytics" tab to see charts of your strength progression.
*   **Update Plans:** Modify your existing routines as you increase your strength. 
*   **Offline Access:** The application stores data locally. It syncs the information when you reconnect to the internet.
*   **Security:** Your workout data stays private. The platform encrypts all entries before syncing them to the cloud.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Does the software require a paid subscription?**
No. This software is free for individual gym users.

**Can I export my workout data?**
Yes. Navigate to the settings menu and select "Export Data" to download your logs in a spreadsheet format.

**What happens if I forget my password?**
Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a reset code at your registered email address.

**Does this work on mobile devices?**
This version is designed for Windows desktop browsers. A mobile application is planned for future updates.

**How do I clear my local cache?**
Go to Settings, select "System," and choose "Clear Cache" if the application slows down or displays outdated information.
